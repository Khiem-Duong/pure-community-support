Where it comes from

Funded by sales, reported in public

Pure earns its community funding only by selling traditional Hue handicrafts. It does not ask for money, and it declines every offer of it.

After operating costs, half of net profit goes to community support and half is reinvested in product development. All funds are held in one bank account opened only for this purpose.
