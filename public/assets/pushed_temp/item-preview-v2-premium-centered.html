Ceremonial Gong

Hand-forged bronze, hammered to shape and tuned entirely by ear in Hue.

On request Pricing confirmed at inquiry

In stock — ready to ship

This gong follows a form used in Hue ceremonial and meditative practice for generations. A single strike holds its note for close to a minute, settling a room the way nothing else quite does.

Because each piece is worked by hand, no two share exactly the same tone. Small variations in the hammer marks and patina are inherent to the process, not defects in it.
