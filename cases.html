Half of the net profit from every Pure sale funds community programmes in Hue. This page sets out each programme: its purpose, its current status and the funds it has received. Figures are updated every quarter.

Support for individuals and families

A case records the support provided to one family or individual: who received it, what it covered and the total amount. A case may stand alone or form part of a campaign.

How the programmes are funded

Pure's revenue comes from product sales only. After operating costs, 50% of net profit is allocated to community programmes in Hue; the other half is reinvested into the artisans and the catalog.
