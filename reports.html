How we report

Every recap shows the same five things

Pure publishes a recap on the blog every month or quarter. Each one follows the same structure, so figures can be compared from one period to the next. All amounts come from the single bank account opened only for this project.

  1. Total revenue from product sales in the period.
  2. Operating costs, such as materials, artisan payments, shipping and running the site.
  3. Net profit, the amount left after operating costs.
  4. The 50/50 split between community support and product development.
  5. Where the community share went, by program and general support.

The annual impact report

Once a year, Pure will publish a formal report that brings together the year's figures and programs in one document. The first edition is in preparation. Its publication date will be announced here.

  • Full-year revenue, operating costs and net profit
  • The community share, broken down by program
  • Progress and follow-up for each running program
  • Artisans and collaborators who contributed during the year
